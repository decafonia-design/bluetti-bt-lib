FAQ

Good to know.

How do I order?

Use the order form on this site or email info@3deca.com. I'll confirm your order and send payment details.

Are prices with or without VAT?

All prices are ex VAT. Contact me if you need a commercial invoice.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping within the EU is free on orders above €130. For smaller orders or shipping outside the EU, ask and I'll let you know.

Can you make a custom size or fit?

Yes. Storage boxes and caddies come in different bay counts, and I can make inserts for your case. Send me your setup.

What are the products made of?

Sturdy carbon / nylon. The Euronorm case support for the Sennheiser caddy is made of ABS.
